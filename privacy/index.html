Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy covers debug.marian.online, the booking page for Debug Sessions run by Marian Montagnino ("I", "me"). It explains what data is collected, why, and the choices you have.

What I collect

  • Booking details. When you book, the scheduling service collects your name, email, chosen time, and your answers to the intake questions about your bug. I use these to prepare for and run your session, send your summary, and handle refunds.
  • Payment. Payments are processed by Stripe through the scheduling service. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage and advertising data. This site uses the Reddit Pixel and the Meta Pixel (and may use Google's tag) to measure visits and count completed bookings from ads. These tools set cookies or similar identifiers and receive information such as pages viewed, your browser and device, your IP address, and whether a booking was completed.

What happens on the call

You share your screen and stay in control. I don't log into your accounts, repositories or production systems. Sessions are not recorded unless you agree first. Please don't show customer personal data or secrets on screen.

How I use your data

  • To schedule, run and follow up on your session, and to issue refunds.
  • To measure which ads lead to bookings, so I spend less on ads that don't work.
  • I don't sell your personal information, and I don't share your intake answers with advertisers.

Services that process data

The scheduling service (Cal.com or Calendly), Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), Reddit, Meta and, if enabled, Google (ad measurement), and the video-call provider named in your invite. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser, or use a content blocker, and the site still works.
  • You can manage ad personalization in your Reddit, Meta and Google account settings.
  • You can ask me to see, correct or delete the booking data I hold about you, subject to records I must keep for tax and payment purposes.

How long I keep it

Booking details and session summaries are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and meet tax and accounting obligations, then deleted.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact me through marian.online.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
